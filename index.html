Topics of Interest

Horses

Since I was 10 years old, horses have been a very important part of my life. They bring me peace and give me a purpose. I've been active in multiple disciplines of equestrian sports (dressage, carriage driving, and just having fun). I am currently learning a new part of the horse world: Western and horse(wo)manship! I’m also expecting my first foal in June 2026!

💻

Tech

I have always been a proud geek with an interest in technology. But my recent discovery of computer science still came as a surprise! My natural curiosity led me to discover the incredible potential of AI and Computer Science. I truly believe AI is one of the most powerful tools of our time—if you use it well. That realization has inspired me to make a bold move: a career switch from healthcare to IT. I’m excited to bring my unique perspective and my interest in humanity to the tech world and see where this journey takes me!

✨

Spirituality

During my self-development and mental health journey over the last few years, I’ve discovered that I am a highly spiritual person (if I set my dark sense of humor aside for a moment). For as long as I can remember, I’ve been a 'walking head'—feeling like the walking dead—always insecure and stuck in negative self-talk. Working on becoming the best version of myself, reading, and building my life according to my key values has helped me truly stand for who I am becoming. It has finally allowed me to get in touch with my true self!
