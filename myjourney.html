My Journey


My name is Joëlle, and I am 22 years old. Over the past few years, I have transformed my life from the inside out. By discovering my true identity and building a life based on my core values, I have grown from an insecure girl into a capable, resilient woman. This journey has proven to me that you can truly change your life if you are willing to believe in yourself and put in the work.

I am a lifelong learner, and this mindset is what eventually led me to the world of technology. I am fascinated by Data Science, as it allows me to combine my technical interest with my curiosity about human behavior and psychology. To me, data is not just numbers; it is a reflection of how we live and interact.

My goal is to use my unique perspective—shaped by my background in healthcare and my personal growth—to bridge the gap between complex data and human understanding. I am currently building my future in tech, one step at a time. You can find my completed projects and the code I am currently working on on my GitHub profile. I am excited to see how I can contribute to the world of AI and Machine Learning.

Love, Jo
