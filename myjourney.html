My Journey


My name is Joëlle, and I am a 22-year-old woman from the Netherlands. Through self-study and reading over the past year, I have been able to completely change my life from the inside out. I’ve discovered my true identity and started making choices based on my own values. This has had a major impact on my life; I can honestly say that I feel genuinely happy with myself now.

Even though I am still learning every single day and continue to develop myself, I have grown from an insecure kid into a capable, strong, and resilient woman. This has proven to me that you can change your life if you are willing to believe in it!

Through my blog, I want to take you along on my journey and the road ahead. Occasionally, I’ll take a trip down memory lane to show you where I came from and how I managed to turn my life around. I hope you are as excited as I am, so hop on and enjoy the ride!

Love, Jo
